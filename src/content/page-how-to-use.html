Guide

How to use Vivi.

From idea to published video in under 10 minutes.

1
Write your story idea

Type a description in the creation box or the Studio. Anything from one sentence to a full paragraph. Vivi reads your intent and builds from it. You can also paste a link to a news article or Wikipedia page.

💡 Tip: Be specific. The more texture you give, the more cinematic the result.
2
Choose your visual style

Vivi suggests a style based on your story type. Available styles: Pixar 3D, Indian Ghibli, Cinematic Realistic, 2D Animation, Watercolour.

3
Review the script (optional)

Before generating visuals, Vivi shows you the script. Edit dialogue, change scene order, or accept as-is. Creator and Pro users can upload their own voice for narration here.

4
Wait for generation (4-8 min)

Vivi generates your video: visuals, consistent characters, voice, music. You will get a notification when ready. Pro users get instant generation.

💡 You do not need to stay on the page. We will notify you.
5
Download or publish directly

Download as MP4 or publish directly to YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram. Subtitles auto-generated in 12 languages on Creator and Pro. You own the video, use it commercially, no attribution required.
